## Design (from the abstract)

- One task: a contract-based migration of a Go file signer from RSA-2048 PKCS#1 v1.5 to Pure ML-DSA-44, checked by 40 checks.
- One binary factor: **checker access vs none**. Both conditions get the contract, compiler, documentation and OpenSSL.
- 160 attempts across 4 local-agent configurations.

## Results (from the abstract)

- 12 patches pass locally but fail the external requirements.
- Checker access raised completion in no comparison.
- Cutting the context window from 128K to 32K dropped full passes from 36/40 to 4/40.
